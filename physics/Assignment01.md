# Computational Physics — Important Questions & Answers

## Part A: Short Answer Questions

### 1. Define Computational Physics in one sentence.
Computational Physics is the use of computers, numerical methods, and algorithms to solve and simulate complex physical problems.

### 2. Name any one area where Density Functional Theory (DFT) is applied.
DFT is applied in **materials science** to study the electronic structure and properties of materials.

### 3. Which branch of Computational Physics is used in antenna design?
**Computational Electromagnetics (CEM)** is used in antenna design.

### 4. Expand FEM.
**FEM = Finite Element Method.**

### 5. What role does Computer Science play in Computational Physics?
Computer Science provides **programming, algorithms, data structures, and computational tools** for solving physical problems.

### 6. Give one difference between analytical and computational methods.
- **Analytical:** Gives an exact mathematical solution.
- **Computational:** Uses numerical methods and computers to obtain an approximate solution.

### 7. State one advantage of simulations over real experiments.
Simulations are **safer and cheaper** because experiments can be performed virtually without physical equipment.

### 8. Which programming language is most commonly used in Computational Physics?
**Python** is one of the most commonly used programming languages in Computational Physics.

# Part B: Conceptual Questions

## 1. Explain how Computational Physics acts as a bridge between Physics, Mathematics, and Computer Science.

Computational Physics combines:
- **Physics** – provides physical laws and real-world problems.
- **Mathematics** – provides equations and numerical methods.
- **Computer Science** – provides programming, algorithms, and computational tools.

Thus, Computational Physics uses mathematical models and computer programs to solve complex physical problems.

## 2. Write a short note on the role of Molecular Dynamics (MD) simulations in Materials Science.

Molecular Dynamics (MD) is a computer simulation method used to study the motion and interaction of atoms and molecules. It helps analyze **material properties, atomic structure, temperature effects, and mechanical behavior** without performing costly physical experiments.

## 3. Differentiate between Computational Electromagnetics (CEM) and Finite Element Modeling (FEM).

| CEM | FEM |
|---|---|
| Used to solve electromagnetic problems. | A numerical method used to solve differential equations. |
| Mainly used for antennas, wave propagation, and electromagnetic fields. | Used in structural, thermal, fluid, and electromagnetic problems. |
| Focuses specifically on electromagnetics. | Can be applied to many engineering and physics problems. |

## 4. How is Quantum Computing connected to Computational Physics? Give one Computer Science application.

Quantum Computing uses the principles of quantum mechanics to perform computations. It can help solve complex physics problems such as **quantum systems and molecular simulations**.

**Computer Science application:** Cryptography.

## 5. Why is Computational Physics important for CS/IT students?

Computational Physics is important for CS/IT students because it develops skills in **programming, algorithms, numerical methods, simulation, and data analysis**, which are useful in scientific computing, AI, engineering, and research.

## 6. Describe two advantages of using virtual laboratories (simulations) in science and engineering.

### 1. Cost Effective
Virtual laboratories reduce the need for expensive equipment, materials, and laboratory setups.

### 2. Safe and Repeatable
Students can perform experiments safely and repeat them multiple times without risk of damaging equipment or causing accidents.
